# Pass 3D handoff — evidence before expansion

Pass 3C does not establish that the game is entertaining. This plan distinguishes working strategic choices from missing ones. No proposed 3D feature below was implemented during 3C.

## Evidence and encounter assessment

Evidence: `pass3c-marius.json`, `pass3c-switch.json`, `pass3c-withdrawn.json` in `docs/evidence` record seven-night Sandbox routes, per-night resources, opportunities and finalized receipts. Browser Marius route used appointment → hunt → hunt → Mirelle → publication → withdrawal → hunt. Sanctuary/switch route used title → sanctuary → hunt → Mirelle → withdrawal → Dorian → final publication.

**Strongest:** withdrawing Mirelle before publication and switching to Dorian. The invitation and Cassian's derived route actually disappear; sanctuary buys the time needed for a replacement investigation. The alternative publication cannot finish before Night VII. This combines ownership, finite time, a rival request and independently surviving shelter.

**Three weakest major encounters:**

1. **Final reckoning after early consolidation.** Marius route resolved service through research on V, withdrew access on VI, and hunted on VII. There was no remaining political antagonist or consequential confrontation. Outcome explanation improved, but not the underlying climax.
2. **Marius's direct service.** One action and one Blood fulfil an abstract institutional duty. Research is a better integrated alternative, but the direct task has no playable subject or negotiation. Do not invent historical canon to fill it.
3. **Seraphine's sanctuary bargain after acceptance.** It is mechanically valuable and irreversible for its fixed term, but payment ends almost all strategic interaction with her. There is no scarcity, competing investment or later demand generated by the price. Its reliability is legitimate; manufactured retaliation would violate its terms.

Other weak agency: Vesper's scheduled withdrawal can be anticipated but cannot be negotiated with or politically challenged. Dorian petitions but cannot independently seize the player's Library. Research publishes an abstract account of current obligations, not actionable secret evidence. Sabine's sponsorship benefits Cassian but offers the player little counterplay. Inspecting genealogy is informative, not a solution to opposition.

## Strategic gaps

- Player initiative is selecting existing finite opportunities, not making a proposal to another actor.
- No supported use of discovered information for blackmail, bargaining, public challenge or changing an opponent's options. Private knowledge must remain actor-scoped if this is extended.
- Finished research closes its rival permanently; retaining versus withdrawing an already-published investigator's key changes authority but barely changes subsequent incentives.
- Patron grants remain revocable in law, but after earned protection most policies do not threaten withdrawal. Do not confuse a possible contract revocation with implemented political danger.
- Refusal of ordinary agreements has no invented penalty; it also rarely changes the next conversation. New reactions must follow recorded refusal, not merely closing dialogue.
- Blood creates deadline pressure initially, then scheduled feeding. In tested sheltered routes Exposure recedes rapidly. More resource bookkeeping would not solve the absence of opposition.

## Political economy

**Missing prerequisite:** no NPC purchasing treasury, bounded payment proposal, NPC-to-NPC purchase, shortage response or negotiated price exists. Seraphine collecting player-paid escrow is real, but is not an NPC-to-NPC purchase. Pass 3C's requested demonstration is therefore unavailable. Existing restricted function-call keys must not be replaced with unrestricted wallets to fill this gap.

The player starts with disposable Sandbox execution funding. The 0.02 NEAR covenant competes mainly for early actions, not scarce investment capital. No useful alternative purchase creates a financial opportunity cost. Lounge entry is already invited in new Living Court campaigns; the preserved legacy Lounge rental is not a new profitable property market. Library title enables actual invitations and research, but creates no earnings. Native account balances, game Blood, escrow and fictional wealth are separate concepts.

Before adding purchases, specify a small contract-enforced funded spending capability, exact recipient/method/budget/nonce/expiry rules and failure recovery. Existing atomic lease acceptance escrows payment and enables access in one receipt; payout is a separate asynchronous step. A new economic feature must prove its own settlement guarantees. This is a separately scoped security change, not a narrative patch.

## Campaign progression

I–III offer a concrete survival deadline and competing shelter routes. IV–V are strongest when researcher choice can displace direct service or close a rival route. VI–VII differ sharply: the switch route still has publication at stake; early consolidation can leave only upkeep and optional key withdrawal. Permanently lost opportunities should remain lost; replace empty nights only with consequences of actual decisions.

There is no implemented succession contest despite Marius's fictional domain, no final institutional challenge and no way to turn published findings into leverage against a patron. Victory currently means qualifying shelter/protection and survival, not defeating a political adversary. Ending prose must not pretend otherwise. Seraphine's temporary refuge justifies Unbound for the current horizon, not permanent freedom or inherited property.

Implement one coherent slice at a time. First choose the central climax and the exact economy scope; do not implement every row as a large simulator. Preserve old journals and saves, the documented one-major-action rule unless intentionally redesigned, independent founders, approved portraits and real authorization boundaries. Re-run contrasting seven-night routes and evaluate whether opposition and counterplay improved, not merely whether more scenes appeared.
