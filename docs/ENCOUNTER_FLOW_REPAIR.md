# Circular encounter-flow repair

Status: implementation complete locally; browser acceptance BLOCKED. No commit, push or deployment.

## Causes and bounded corrections
- `livingCourt` generated `ask:<night>:<actor>` and `personal:<night>:<actor>` for unchanged answers. `VampireEncounter` used destination IDs from `seen` for every link, including Back and proposal navigation. `encounters.ts` now derives stable topic IDs and versions only displayed title/dialogue/material terms. Resource totals, whole snapshots, route and night are not read-status keys.
- `EncounterDirector` now records the displayed version once its information is visible. Known static legacy answers retain their read status; conditional legacy answers with no recoverable version say **Review needed**, not falsely **Updated** or completed. The existing campaign-scoped localStorage key and seen history remain. New versioned records distinguish New, Updated and Explored.
- `VampireEncounter` moves read informational questions to Review conversation, keeps unresolved proposals available, and folds an unchanged character introduction on return. Navigation and executable actions have no read badge. A live proposal's current terms remain visible before commitment.
- `repairCourtFlow` in `livingCourt.ts` consolidates Discovery, the nightly incident and the nightly choice menu into one current situation/decision surface. Old scene IDs are non-executing redirects, preserving saved navigation. The redundant hold/wait confirmation also redirects there; its single end-night control includes upkeep and timing.
- Negotiations show their unique dialogue, material terms and explicit commitment together, without Continue gates. Investigator purpose remains an optional question, not a second mandatory statement. Information buttons say Discuss/Review; executable buttons state the commitment and nightly action/Blood/payment cost.
- The final demand is one decision scene containing eligible direct responses, each with its distinct consequences. Nightly, Marius and old final-action routes resolve there. Feed and intentionally leaving it unanswered remain valid existing alternatives. Settled final routes lead to Marius's current situation, never another execution.
- The unconditional global Feed caused duplicate controls in nightly choices and the Feed confirmation. It is now omitted when that scene already has a feeding entry or is the dedicated Feed scene. Feeding remains repeatable according to engine availability.
- Marius's questions/opening follow his own effective grant, outstanding/fulfilled service and final answer. Old recruitment comparison is review material after the relationship exists. Seraphine acknowledges independent shelter, accepted sanctuary and expired terms. Investigators distinguish authorization, underway work, required later reckoning and publication. Blocked proposals display existing availability reasons and the existing withdrawal path where eligible. Direct service does not suppress otherwise legitimate research.
- Existing commit lock, current-scene resolution, campaign revision and server validation remain. Pending/error views have no executable commitments. Safe error navigation and Check confirmed outcome use the existing `view` recovery path; confirmed refresh clears the stale action view. Dismissing history or a result never signs or advances another night. Legacy manual-night feedback does not claim an automatic reckoning.

No NPC policy, action availability rule, contract method, campaign state/schema, economic rule, deadline, lifecycle, credential or funding change in this task. Existing independent rights and paid sanctuary semantics remain. Prior local Narrative V2 and investigation/finale changes were preserved; only targeted state-aware copy, duplication and control wording were adjusted.
